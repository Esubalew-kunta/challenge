## Sections

> **Every section names its material.** A section whose instruction is "explain our approach
> to X" is a slop generator with a ticket number. Pin it to a row in the brief's material
> ledger, or mark it `blocked` and go get the material.
>
> Sections should be load-bearing and sequential — if they can be shuffled with nothing
> breaking, this is coverage, not an argument (Rule 3.7). Say what each one *needs from the
> one before*.
