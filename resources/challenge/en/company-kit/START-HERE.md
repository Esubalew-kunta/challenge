# Day 30 Company Kit

Move from individual experiments to a small, governed team system.

## What this kit solves

Teams often lose value because instructions are inconsistent, nobody owns shared assets, risky permissions spread, and completed work is difficult to hand off. This kit provides a practical baseline without an AI API or daily monitoring process.

## Start in 30 minutes

1. Name one adoption owner and one security reviewer.
2. Complete `TEAM-SCORE.md` together.
3. Adapt `COMPANY-CLAUDE-TEMPLATE.md` with real policies.
4. Review `SECURITY-CHECKLIST.md` before copying the included `.claude` folder.
5. Pilot with one team and two low risk workflows using `ADOPTION-ROADMAP.md`.

Keep publishing, deletion, payments, access changes, legal commitments, and sensitive data decisions under human approval.
