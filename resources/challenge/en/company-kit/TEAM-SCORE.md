# Team readiness score

Give the team one point for each yes.

1. The pilot has one business outcome.
2. An adoption owner is named.
3. A security reviewer is named.
4. Allowed projects and data are documented.
5. Human approval boundaries are clear.
6. Permissions follow minimum access.
7. External resources are inspected and licensed.
8. Shared assets have owners and review dates.
9. New users complete a safe practice task.
10. Handoffs include evidence and remaining risk.
11. Unattended work has limits and stop conditions.
12. The pilot compares results with a baseline.

0 to 5: not ready to expand. Complete the missing controls.

6 to 9: ready for a limited pilot. Keep the scope small.

10 to 12: ready for measured expansion, one team or workflow at a time.
