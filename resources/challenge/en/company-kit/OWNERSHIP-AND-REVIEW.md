# Ownership and review cadence

A shared asset without an owner becomes stale company risk.

## Required record

For every company instruction, rule, skill, hook, plugin, and MCP connection, record:

1. Business purpose
2. Named owner
3. Approved users and data
4. Source and license when external
5. Verification method
6. Last review date
7. Next review date
8. Removal method

## Lightweight review cadence

After the pilot: review evidence and remove anything unused.

Monthly during expansion: review new assets, permissions, incidents, and repeated friction.

Quarterly after the system is stable: confirm owners, access, sources, licenses, and business value.

Immediately after a security event, major product change, or owner departure: review affected assets and revoke unneeded access.

No daily monitoring meeting is required for this baseline. Assign clear alerts and escalation only where company risk requires them.
