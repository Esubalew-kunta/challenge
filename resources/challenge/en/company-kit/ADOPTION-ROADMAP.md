# Lightweight adoption roadmap

Use a small pilot. Do not require daily monitoring or a large platform project.

## Stage 1: Prepare

Name the owner, security reviewer, approved project, allowed data, forbidden actions, and two measurable outcomes. Record the current time and error rate as a baseline.

## Stage 2: Pilot

Choose three to five users from one team. Train them on instructions, permissions, verification, and handoffs. Begin with planning, drafting, analysis, or low risk code changes.

## Stage 3: Standardize

Keep only the instructions and skills that produced evidence of value. Assign an owner and review date to each shared asset. Remove duplicates and unused connections.

## Stage 4: Expand carefully

Add one team or workflow at a time. Compare quality, time, errors, and user confidence with the baseline. Expand only when security controls and results remain clear.

## Stop signal

Pause expansion if ownership is unclear, sensitive data appears in the wrong place, permissions exceed the task, or verification cannot prove the result.
