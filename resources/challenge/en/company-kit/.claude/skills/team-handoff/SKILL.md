# Team handoff

Use this when work moves to another person, session, or team.

## Gather

Collect the requested outcome, actual changes or decisions, verification evidence, approvals, open work, known risks, and relevant files. Mark missing facts clearly.

## Check

1. Separate completed work from planned work.
2. Never claim a check passed without evidence.
3. Name actions that still need human approval.
4. Identify the next smallest safe action.
5. Assign the next owner or state that no owner is known.
6. Add a stop condition for new risk or scope.

## Output

Return: Outcome, Completed, Evidence, Not completed, Risks, Approvals, Next action, Next owner, Stop condition, References.

Do not expose secrets or unnecessary personal data in the handoff.
