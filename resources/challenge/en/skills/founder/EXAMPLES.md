# Examples

## Beginner

```text
/idea-validator Challenge this scheduling idea. Separate evidence from assumptions and design one cheap experiment for the biggest unknown.
```

```text
/decision-memo Help me choose between hiring now and waiting one quarter. Include doing nothing, tradeoffs, risk, reversibility, and missing evidence.
```

```text
/founder-weekly-review Turn these notes and metrics into a short review. Give me one priority, three supporting moves, and one thing to stop next week.
```

## Builder

```text
/idea-validator Compare this idea with the user's current alternatives. Rank assumptions across demand, distribution, delivery, and viability. Define evidence thresholds and stop conditions.
```

```text
/decision-memo Build a board ready memo with decision criteria, options, second order effects, reversible steps, confidence, dissent, and a review trigger.
```

```text
/founder-weekly-review Compare planned outcomes with actual evidence across customers, product, team, and cash. Show stale inputs and decisions that cannot wait.
```
