# Decision memo

Make the choice easier to evaluate without pretending uncertainty has disappeared.

## Frame the decision

Confirm the decision, owner, deadline, reason it matters now, and consequence of doing nothing.

## Build the memo

1. Context and verified evidence.
2. Decision criteria in priority order.
3. Real options, including delay or no action when valid.
4. Benefits, costs, and tradeoffs for each option.
5. Risks, dependencies, and second order effects.
6. What is reversible and what is difficult to reverse.
7. Recommendation with confidence and reasoning.
8. Strongest argument against the recommendation.
9. Decision owner, actions, communication, and review trigger.

Label assumptions and missing evidence. Do not create consensus, financial facts, legal claims, or commitments that were not supplied.
