# Founder weekly review

Create a short review from supplied customer notes, metrics, product updates, team updates, and financial facts.

## Review

1. Company health: what improved, weakened, or stayed uncertain and why.
2. Customer signals: behavior, feedback, losses, and repeated needs.
3. Progress: outcomes completed compared with the week's priorities.
4. Misses: what did not happen and the likely cause.
5. Product and delivery: shipped value, quality, and bottlenecks.
6. Team: capacity, ownership, and important unresolved issues.
7. Cash or runway only when reliable inputs were supplied.
8. Risks: likelihood, impact, mitigation, and owner.
9. Decisions: choice, owner, evidence needed, and deadline.
10. Next week: one main outcome, up to three moves, and one thing to stop.

Separate facts, interpretation, and recommendation. Mark any missing or stale input. Do not invent company health, customer signals, revenue, runway, or team sentiment.
