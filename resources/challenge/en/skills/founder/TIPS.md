# Tips

## Compound founder judgment

After each important decision, record what you believed, what evidence you used, and what result appeared. When the same blind spot returns, improve one skill question or decision criterion.

1. Save the original assumption and confidence.
2. Define the evidence that could change your mind.
3. Review the result on a real date.
4. Separate luck from a repeatable signal.
5. Add one reusable lesson to the relevant skill.

Put stable company facts and decision principles in `CLAUDE.md`. Put repeatable review methods in skills. Keep temporary market claims and private people data in the authorized task context.

Good founder systems do not remove judgment. They make evidence, uncertainty, ownership, and learning easier to see.
