# Founder Skills Pack

These skills reduce noise around three recurring founder jobs: validate demand, make a difficult decision, and choose the next priority.

## Choose the right skill

- `/idea-validator` tests a target user, painful problem, evidence, alternatives, risky assumptions, and the next experiment.
- `/decision-memo` makes options, criteria, tradeoffs, risk, and reversibility visible.
- `/founder-weekly-review` turns company evidence into health, customer signals, risks, decisions, and next week priorities.

## Beginner path

Use one skill for one decision. Supply only facts you can verify and ask the skill to mark every assumption.

## Builder path

Add source notes, metric definitions, decision deadlines, constraints, and explicit stop conditions. Compare the recommendation with the strongest opposing case.

The skills require explicit invocation. They do not contact customers, commit spending, publish, or make a decision on your behalf.
