# Examples

## Beginner

```text
/discovery-brief Turn these interview notes into a brief. Separate client statements from assumptions and give me the five most valuable next questions.
```

```text
/proposal-builder Draft a short proposal from the approved discovery brief. Leave visible placeholders for missing price and dates. Do not invent proof or commitments.
```

```text
/meeting-to-action-plan Turn these meeting notes into decisions, actions, owners, due dates, and open questions. Mark anything that was discussed but not agreed.
```

## Builder

```text
/discovery-brief Compare interviews from leadership and frontline users. Show where evidence agrees, conflicts, or remains missing. Rank questions by their effect on scope.
```

```text
/proposal-builder Build a two phase engagement with measurable acceptance criteria, dependencies, assumptions, exclusions, and a decision path. Use only supplied commercial facts.
```

```text
/meeting-to-action-plan Create a client ready action plan, but separate the private risk notes into a section that must not be sent externally.
```
