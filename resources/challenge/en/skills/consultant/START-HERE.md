# Consultant Skills Pack

These skills turn messy client information into clearer decisions without inventing facts or commitments.

## Choose the right skill

- `/discovery-brief` organizes evidence, pain points, stakeholders, unknowns, and next questions.
- `/proposal-builder` turns validated discovery into outcomes, scope, deliverables, assumptions, and exclusions.
- `/meeting-to-action-plan` separates agreed decisions from suggestions and gives every real action an owner and due date.

## Beginner path

Start with discovery, validate the brief with the client, then build a proposal. After every meeting, create an action plan and ask participants to confirm unclear items.

## Builder path

Provide source notes, approved facts, the decision being made, and the required output format. Ask the skill to label evidence, inference, and missing information.

The skills require explicit invocation and do not send, publish, price, or sign anything.
