# Proposal builder

Draft a decision ready proposal from the supplied discovery brief and approved commercial facts.

## Confirm the foundation

Identify the client outcome, evidence, decision criteria, budget information, timing, and approver. Mark anything missing instead of guessing.

## Draft the proposal

Include:

1. Client outcome and why it matters.
2. Current problem using verified evidence.
3. Recommended approach and the reason it fits.
4. Scope with included work.
5. Deliverables with acceptance criteria.
6. Timeline with dependencies and client responsibilities.
7. Assumptions that affect delivery.
8. Exclusions that protect both sides from unclear scope.
9. Investment only when approved pricing was supplied.
10. Next steps, owner, and decision date.

Use clear language and make commitments measurable. Do not invent testimonials, results, credentials, pricing, legal terms, or client approval. Mark all placeholders visibly.
