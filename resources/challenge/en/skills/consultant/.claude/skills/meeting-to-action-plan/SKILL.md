# Meeting to action plan

Create a useful record from the supplied notes or transcript. Do not send it or update another system.

## Separate certainty

Distinguish what was agreed, what was suggested, and what remains unclear. Never turn discussion into a commitment.

## Return

1. Purpose and participants.
2. Key facts and context.
3. Decisions that were explicitly agreed.
4. Actions with action, owner, due date, and completion evidence.
5. Open questions with the person best placed to answer.
6. Risks, dependencies, and unresolved disagreement.
7. Follow up agenda and next meeting trigger.

Use `owner not assigned` or `due date not agreed` when needed. End with a short confirmation list showing which decisions or actions need participant validation.
