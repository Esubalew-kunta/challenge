# SOP builder

Create a procedure people can follow and verify without filling gaps by guesswork.

## Establish the process

Confirm the purpose, trigger, owner, audience, inputs, tools, output, frequency, and constraints. Separate the process observed today from any proposed improvement.

## Build the SOP

Include:

1. Purpose and scope.
2. Owner and participating roles.
3. Required inputs, access, and prerequisites.
4. Trigger and expected completion time.
5. Numbered steps with action, owner, output, and completion evidence.
6. Decision points with clear conditions and paths.
7. Exceptions with safe recovery and escalation.
8. Controls for quality, privacy, permissions, and approval.
9. Final evidence that proves the process finished correctly.
10. Version owner and review date.

Do not include real credentials or assume access. Mark any unverified step and ask a process owner to test the SOP before adoption.
