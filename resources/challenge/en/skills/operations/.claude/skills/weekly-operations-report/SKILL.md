# Weekly operations report

Turn verified weekly inputs into a report that helps the reader decide what needs attention.

## Validate inputs

Confirm the reporting period, metric source, comparison period, incident status, owners, and data gaps. Never invent a number or status.

## Report

1. Service health: overall state and the evidence behind it.
2. Volume and performance: supplied measures, comparison, and meaningful change.
3. Completed work and positive outcomes.
4. Incidents: impact, current status, owner, recovery, and learning.
5. Blockers and dependencies.
6. Risks: likelihood, impact, mitigation, and owner.
7. Decisions needed: decision, options, owner, and required date.
8. Next week: top priorities and expected evidence of progress.
9. Data gaps and confidence limits.

Keep sensitive details out unless the intended audience is authorized. Separate facts, interpretation, and recommendation.
