# Operations Skills Pack

These skills make repeatable work easier to run, inspect, and improve without needing a paid integration.

## Choose the right skill

- `/sop-builder` turns a real process into steps, owners, decision points, exceptions, controls, and completion evidence.
- `/process-auditor` finds bottlenecks, failure modes, weak controls, and improvement priorities.
- `/weekly-operations-report` turns verified weekly inputs into service health, incidents, risks, decisions, and next week priorities.

## Beginner path

Document one small process first. Ask its owner to test every step. Audit it only after the current process is accurately represented.

## Builder path

Provide process evidence, metrics with sources, known controls, incident records, and the decision the output must support.

The skills require explicit invocation. They do not change live systems, disable controls, send reports, or expose credentials.
