# Tips

## Compound operational knowledge

After each real run, compare the documented process with what actually happened. When the same exception or correction appears again, update the relevant skill or SOP with one tested improvement.

1. Observe the current process.
2. Record evidence and variation.
3. Find one repeated gap.
4. Make one controlled change.
5. Measure the result before standardizing it.

Keep stable operating rules in `CLAUDE.md`, repeatable methods in skills, and process specific details in the protected project.

Do not automate a broken process merely because it repeats. First remove unclear ownership, unnecessary steps, and unsafe access.
