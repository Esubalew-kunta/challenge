# Performance review

Turn supplied results into learning and a next experiment without overstating causation.

## Validate the measurement

Confirm the objective, metric definition, target, actual result, source, comparison period, segments, spend, and tracking changes. Mark stale or missing inputs.

## Review

1. Result: target compared with actual and meaningful difference.
2. Funnel or journey: where performance changed.
3. Segments and channels: useful variation, not vanity ranking.
4. Creative and message signals.
5. Cost and efficiency when reliable inputs exist.
6. Attribution: what is observed, what is inferred, and what cannot be claimed.
7. Data gaps, tracking risk, and confidence.
8. Lessons to keep, stop, or investigate.
9. Next experiment: hypothesis, audience, change, success measure, guardrail, and stop condition.

Do not claim causation from correlation. Do not hide weak results or select only favorable metrics.
