# Campaign brief

Create a brief that aligns the team before content or media work begins.

## Confirm inputs

Identify the audience, customer evidence, business context, approved offer, brand constraints, timing, budget information, channels, and approvers. Mark missing items.

## Build the brief

1. Audience: segment, situation, need, and current behavior.
2. Insight: the evidence based reason the message may matter.
3. Objective: one primary change in behavior or perception.
4. Offer: value, conditions, and approved proof.
5. Message: main promise, supporting points, and claim evidence.
6. Call to action: one clear next step.
7. Channel role: why each selected channel belongs in the journey.
8. Required assets, owners, and approval points.
9. Success measures: primary measure, guardrails, target, source, and review date.
10. Constraints, risks, exclusions, and open questions.

Do not invent audience research, claims, testimonials, targets, prices, or approval. Flag any statement that needs legal, brand, or product review.
