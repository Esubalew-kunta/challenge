# Examples

## Beginner

```text
/campaign-brief Build a brief for this webinar. Use one audience, one objective, one message, one call to action, and one primary success measure. Mark missing evidence.
```

```text
/content-repurposer Turn this approved article into one LinkedIn post and one email. Preserve every factual claim and use only the supplied call to action.
```

```text
/performance-review Review this campaign against its target. Separate the actual result, interpretation, attribution limits, and next experiment.
```

## Builder

```text
/campaign-brief Build a multi channel launch brief with channel roles, message hierarchy, claim evidence, asset owners, approval gates, guardrail measures, and risks.
```

```text
/content-repurposer Create channel specific assets for awareness, consideration, and conversion. Trace every claim to the source and flag angles the source cannot support.
```

```text
/performance-review Analyze performance by audience, channel, creative, and funnel stage. Account for tracking changes and define one controlled follow up experiment.
```
