# Marketing Skills Pack

These skills create a simple flow from campaign decision to content production to honest learning.

## Choose the right skill

- `/campaign-brief` aligns audience, objective, offer, message, channel roles, assets, and success measures.
- `/content-repurposer` adapts an approved source truth for each channel while preserving facts and meaning.
- `/performance-review` compares target with actual, exposes attribution and data gaps, and defines the next experiment.

## Beginner path

Write one campaign brief, create content for one or two channels, then review one primary measure. Fewer clear choices beat a large content list with no decision behind it.

## Builder path

Supply audience evidence, approved claims, brand constraints, metric definitions, comparison periods, and tracking limitations.

The skills require explicit invocation. They do not publish, purchase media, contact audiences, or approve claims.
