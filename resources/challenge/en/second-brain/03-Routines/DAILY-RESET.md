# Daily reset

A 7 minute routine for ending confusion and starting with one clear action.

## Minute 1

Open `00_Index.md`. Read the current focus without editing it yet.

## Minutes 2 and 3

Process up to three items from `00-Inbox/`. Move each item to a project, resource, action, or archive decision.

## Minutes 4 and 5

Check active projects. Choose one next action that can be started today. Put it in `00_Index.md`.

## Minute 6

Update any waiting item with a person, expected result, and follow up date.

## Minute 7

Close the loop. Record what changed, what remains open, and the first action for the next session.

Stop after 7 minutes. The goal is clarity, not a perfect workspace.
