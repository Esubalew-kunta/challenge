# Weekly review

A 15 minute routine for keeping the workspace useful without turning it into another job.

## Minutes 1 to 3

Clear the inbox. Decide whether each note belongs in a project, resource, action, or archive.

## Minutes 4 to 7

Review active projects. Confirm the outcome, status, and one next action for each project.

## Minutes 8 to 10

Review waiting items. Add a follow up date or remove the item if it no longer matters.

## Minutes 11 to 13

Review `MEMORY.md`. Keep durable decisions, remove outdated notes, and record one useful lesson.

## Minutes 14 and 15

Choose next week's main focus. Archive completed or inactive material only after confirming it is no longer needed in active folders.

## Finish

- One clear priority is visible in `00_Index.md`.
- Every active project has one next action.
- Nothing important is trapped in the inbox.
