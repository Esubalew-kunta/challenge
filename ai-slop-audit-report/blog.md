# AI-Slop Audit — blog (EN proposed copy)

## Net score: SDS − HPC = 20 − 20(cap) = 0

**Verdict:** Clean (~205 words of in-scope copy; article bodies out of scope). Honest, staked hub. The single negation opener is the only real flag.
**Action:** Ship, but consider reworking the "This isn't X, it's Y" opener — it is the corpus's most-repeated device (see synthesis note). Density reads high only because the copy is short.

## Top tells (verbatim + layer)
- **Negative parallelism opener (Rule 2.1 — THE most reliable tell):** "This isn't a content-marketing blog. It's where we write up what we actually run into shipping AI systems..." Load-bearing and staked (it sets an editorial standard the page then backs up), but structurally the exact 2.1 skeleton. On ~200 words it dominates density.
- **Em-dash triad tail (Rule 2.14 / 2.4, low):** "— the honest agency comparisons we'd want if we were buying, the architecture behind our own internal tools, and where AI training pays off versus where it doesn't." Concrete and specific, so it survives; but it is a rule-of-three after a dash.

## Credits (HPC)
- **Self-imposed standard / stakes (16.2, ~3 cr):** "If a post can't survive our own slop audit, it doesn't go up." A checkable editorial cost the writer holds themselves to — directly load-bearing to the "not content marketing" claim.
- **Admission against interest (16.2, ~2 cr):** "where AI training pays off versus where it doesn't." Concedes training doesn't always pay — spends some sales credibility.
- **Specific address (16.7, ~2 cr):** "the honest agency comparisons we'd want if we were buying." Models a particular reader (a buyer) rather than broadcasting.
- Note (context, not scored): reference article titles show real idiosyncrasy ("the brief that greets our CEO every morning", "every sales call analysed, without taking a note") — but they are out-of-scope calendar copy, not credited here.

## Remediation
- The opener: pick the assertion, drop the negation frame. "This isn't a content-marketing blog. It's where we write up..." → "We write up what we actually run into shipping AI systems: [the three concrete things]." Keeps the substance, removes the corpus tell.

## Repeated devices seen on this page
- **"This isn't X. It's Y." negative parallelism** — the corpus-wide signature. Appears (in inflating, deflating, or scoping variants) on playbook-ia, secteurs, etudes-de-cas, challenge-30-jours, formation-ia and here. This is the top device for synthesis.
- Em-dash + rule-of-three tail.
